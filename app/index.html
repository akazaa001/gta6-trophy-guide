      How this guide is sourced

      Names, descriptions, grades and counts are the official PlayStation 5 list as published, checked against the Exophase trophy list and PSNProfiles. Every guide is written in our own words for this page and names its sources beside it. Where the official description is the only published fact about a trophy, the guide says so instead of inventing a mission name, a location or a step — a cited guide beats an uncited one, however detailed. Missable flags are cautions built from the official descriptions: a trophy is flagged when it is tied to story progress (a partner, a character strand, a story choice), so finish it before the final mission.
